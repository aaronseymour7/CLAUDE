0. How to read this document

  • Pipeline. DMRG (get_energies) → spectrum (get_spectrum) → L-layer approximate trial circuit (mps-to-circuit, L = 1) → filter design (floor.py, certified η) → snapped Trotter grid → counts. Settings are those in pipeline.py: first-order Lie–Trotter, ε targets as given, SWEEP_L = 1, BANDWIDTH = "certified" for the DMRG path, SYNTH_EPS = 1e-3.
  • Two designs per case. Guaranteed = smallest n whose a-priori rigorous bound on 1−F is ≤ ε. Empirical = smallest n whose measured 1−F (against the exact ground state) is ≤ ε. The empirical design uses the exact ground state, so it is an oracle lower envelope, not something a practitioner can know in advance; it needs simulation and is therefore only available for N ≤ 14.
  • Everything uses the DMRG spectrum. E0, E1 (penalty method) and E_top from DMRG; the bandwidth is the certified W = (c_I + Σ|c_β|) − E0, which is 1.6–1.9× larger than E_top − E0 here (XX+YY+ZZ is bounded term by term), so the scaled gap is smaller and T longer (Section 5). The DMRG gap is not certified.
  • N ≤ 14 versus N > 14. For N ≤ 14 an exact-diagonalisation cross-check and statevector simulation of the filter are available: γ is the overlap with the ED ground state, and measured 1−F, P_succ and the state error are reported. For N > 14 there is no ED, no statevector and no dense or sparse 2^N operator: γ is the overlap of the DMRG MPS with the trial circuit simulated as an MPS (Aer matrix-product-state), the energy variance comes from MPO contractions, α is the triangle-inequality constant (Section 5), and circuits are counted, not simulated (no measured 1−F or P_succ; only the rigorous bound).
  • Time. T (scaled) is the total evolution time Σtᵢ in units of 1/W; T (phys) = T/W in units of 1/J1 (H = Σ J S·S, J1 = 1). Pulses with kᵢ = 0 on the grid are dropped.
  • Counting. One Trotter step of one pulse (all bonds, ancilla-controlled) is transpiled once with qiskit (optimization_level=3, basis cx/h/s/rz); filter counts = n × per-step + per pulse (one Rz(2φ), two H). At small n this was checked against a full transpile of the real early-abort circuit (Section 8). RZ counts every rz gate in the basis; RZ (non-Cliff.) only those whose angle is not a multiple of π/2 (these drive the T-count). The T-count is the pipeline’s rough estimate (1.15·log2(1/ε_synth)+9.2 T per rotation, ε_synth = 1e-3); its constants are unverified. Totals include the trial circuit. Connectivity is all-to-all unless marked line (ancilla at the end of a line: pessimistic).
  • Success path. All counts are for the all-zeros (success) path. The expected cost per accepted run is total ÷ P_succ (early abort makes it lower).

5. Effect of the certified bandwidth and of the MPS-only path
